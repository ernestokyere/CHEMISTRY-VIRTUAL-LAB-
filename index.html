EXPERIMENT GUIDE

Determine the Equivalence Point

Add sodium hydroxide (NaOH) gradually to the hydrochloric acid (HCl) sample and observe how the pH changes during neutralization.

1
Prepare

Check the HCl and NaOH concentrations and the sample volume.

2
Titrate

Add NaOH gradually using the burette control.

3
Observe

Watch the pH, indicator, and reaction status as the titration progresses.

4
Analyze

Identify the equivalence point and explain what happened chemically.
